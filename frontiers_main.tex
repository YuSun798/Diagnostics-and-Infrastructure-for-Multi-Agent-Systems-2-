\section*{Ethics Statement}
This review introduces no new human or animal subjects and no new experimental datasets. The retention-focused research agenda raises privacy and governance risks because richer runtime evidence can extend the lifetime and attack surface of user inputs, tool outputs, credentials, and reasoning traces; these risks and mitigation principles are discussed in \S\ref{sec:discussion}.
